Every draw held in **1 of 9** cells. At the tightest request (0.02) the draws held
50%, 72%, 50% - at two of the three corpora the contract is decided by a coin.

**A rate hit in expectation is not a rate bounded with confidence.** That is the
distinction the remedy does not close, and it is not a small one at the requests a
filter is actually set to. Platt ties nothing to the threshold, so its realised rate
has no draw-to-draw variance to report at all: there is one number and it is the
one delivered.
